Just Doodles Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

In short: Just Doodles collects nothing. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no internet access. Your child's drawings stay on your device. The optional PLUS upgrade is paid for through Google Play, and we never see your payment or account details.

Who we are

Just Doodles is a drawing and coloring app for young children, made by an independent developer, DaveenDev. It was built for the developer's own toddler as an alternative to kids' apps full of ads.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, send or share any personal information, from children or from adults. Specifically, it does not:

  • ask for a name, email, age, location or any other details,
  • use accounts or sign-in,
  • show ads or contain advertising code,
  • use analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind,
  • use device identifiers,
  • contain any third-party code, except, in the Google Play version only, Google's Play Billing Library for the optional PLUS upgrade (see below).

The app never has internet access, so it has no way to send anything off your device. The Google Play version asks for two permissions, both needed by Google Play Billing:

  • Google Play billing, which lets the app ask the Play Store app on your device about the PLUS purchase;
  • network state, which only lets it see whether your device is online. It cannot use the network.

Versions of the app from outside Google Play ask for no permissions at all.

The PLUS upgrade

The Google Play version offers one optional, one-time purchase called Just Doodles PLUS. It can only be bought by a grown-up, behind a 2-second hold and a sum to answer, and Google Play handles the whole payment. We never see or store payment details, your name, your email or your Google account. The app only learns from the Play Store app on your device whether PLUS has been bought, and remembers that on your device so PLUS keeps working offline. The purchase itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Drawings and settings

Drawings and settings (such as sounds on or off, and the bedtime timer) are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not included in cloud backups. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

A grown-up can choose to save a drawing to the device or share it with another app. This only happens when a grown-up opens the settings (behind a 2-second hold) and picks a drawing. Android's own "save" and "share" screens handle it, and what happens to a shared picture after that depends on the app it was shared with.

Spoken words

The app says color and picture names out loud using the text-to-speech voice already installed on your device. The app only hands the voice its own short words and phrases, like "Red!" or "Where's the fish?"; it doesn't record or listen to anything. The app has no access to the microphone or camera.

Children

Just Doodles is made for children under 5. Because it collects no personal information, it is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google Play's Families policy. The Google Play version of the app contains no links to websites. Its one optional purchase, PLUS, is only reachable behind the grown-up check; children are never shown ads, prices or offers to buy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change will keep the promise above: no ads, no tracking, nothing collected.
