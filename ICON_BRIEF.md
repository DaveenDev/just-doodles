# App icon brief: Just Doodles

Paste this whole file into the design AI. A short one-paragraph prompt for image generators is at the bottom.

## The app in one paragraph

**Just Doodles** is a drawing and coloring app for toddlers (ages 1 to 4) on Android phones and tablets. A parent made it for their own 2-year-old because every kids' drawing app they tried was full of ads. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no internet access at all, so nothing can pop up and nothing leaves the phone. Children open it, pick a crayon and draw. Nothing in it needs reading: big picture buttons, bright colors, gentle sounds, and a friendly voice that says the names of things out loud.

## Who sees the icon

- **The child (1 to 4 years old)** can't read, so the icon is how they find the app on the home screen. It should be one clear picture a toddler can point at and ask for: "the crayon one."
- **The parent** sees it in the Play Store next to dozens of loud, cluttered kids' apps. It should look calm, trustworthy and handmade, not like a game that sells things.

## What's inside (features)

- **Free Draw**: white or black paper, 12 bright crayons in 3 sizes, stickers, and pattern crayons that draw hearts, stars, zigzags, train tracks and paw prints.
- **Magic crayon**: rainbow, glitter, glow and mirror (draws on both halves, like a butterfly).
- **Color**: 150 coloring pages in 10 groups (animals, sea, garden, things that go, food, sky, farm, bugs, toys, shapes). Tap a shape to fill it, or color by hand.
- **Play**: six gentle games with no scores, no timers and no losing (Peekaboo, Where is it?, a rainbow xylophone, color sorting, feeding hungry animals, following a dotted road).
- **Learn**: colors, shapes, counting to ten, animal sounds, ABC and body parts, all spoken out loud.
- **Baby mode** for 1 to 2-year-olds: every touch blooms shapes and music.
- **Bedtime timer** that puts the app to sleep with a lullaby and a sleepy moon.
- Stamps, eraser, undo, and a confetti celebration when a page is finished.
- Toddler-proof: the Back button can't leave the app or wipe a drawing; grown-up settings hide behind a long press.

## Personality

Warm, playful, simple, safe. Like a fat crayon and a fresh sheet of paper on the kitchen table. Handmade by a parent, not a corporation. Joyful but not loud. The app's tagline in spirit: *"It's just doodles, and that's all it is."*

## The current icon (to be replaced)

A rainbow arching between two small white clouds on a sky-blue (`#7FD3F7`) background. It's cute, but it says "weather" or "generic kids' app", not "drawing". Nothing in it suggests a crayon, a scribble or paper.

## What the new icon must say

At a glance: **drawing, for little kids, happy and safe.** The strongest single idea is a **crayon making a mark**. A scribble, a doodle or a rainbow line coming out of a crayon reads as "drawing" to both a toddler and a parent.

## Directions to explore (pick or combine)

1. **Crayon and scribble**: one fat, rounded crayon drawing a looping, colorful scribble or rainbow squiggle across cream paper.
2. **Happy crayon**: a chunky crayon with a simple smiling face, mid-scribble. A character a child can love.
3. **Doodle sun**: a wobbly, child-drawn smiling sun (uneven rays, like a 3-year-old drew it) with a crayon in the corner. The sun is the app's very first coloring page.
4. **Half-colored picture**: a simple outlined star, heart or flower, half filled in with crayon color, with the crayon still on it. Echoes the Color button in the app.

## Hard requirements

- **No text and no letters**, and no "Free", "No Ads", "Kids" or rating badges (Google Play rejects icons with promotional text or badges).
- **One subject, big and bold.** It must still read at 48 px on a phone's home screen. No tiny details, thin lines or busy backgrounds.
- **Flat shapes with thick rounded outlines**, matching the app. No photorealism, no heavy gradients, no 3D render look, no drop shadows.
- **Android adaptive icon**: 108 × 108 dp canvas, with everything important inside the **center 66 dp circle** (phones crop it to circles, squircles or rounded squares). Deliver the **foreground** (transparent background) and **background** as separate layers.
- **Themed icon**: also a single-color silhouette of the foreground, for Android 13+ themed icons.
- **Google Play icon**: 512 × 512 PNG, full square, no rounded corners or shadow (Play adds its own mask).
- Should work on both light and dark home-screen wallpapers.
- Don't use other brands' looks (no Crayola-style wrapper with stripes and a logo).

## Deliverables

1. Three or four concept sketches, one per direction, shown side by side at both 512 px and 48 px.
2. For the chosen one: a 1024 × 1024 master, the separate foreground and background layers, the monochrome silhouette, and the 512 × 512 Play Store PNG. Vector (SVG) preferred, since the app's current icon is a vector drawable.
